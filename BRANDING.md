## About me

Paste this into Developer Portal → General Information → Description (max 400 characters). Discord shows it as the bot's About me.

```text
🌙 Late-night listening for your server. Lo-fi & chill radio, slowed + reverb, 24/7 rooms. /play to start. Free forever, Premium for 24/7 + filters. Dashboard: krishyaa06.github.io/prism-bots-panel
```
